About

Learned in
other people’s kitchens.

I grew up cooking beside my grandmother in Emilia-Romagna, and spent fourteen years learning what she already knew.

From a family trattoria to Michelin-recognised kitchens in Copenhagen and Lima, I now lead a team of twenty-two in Milan. I care about producers, about staff who stay, and about food that doesn’t need explaining.

01 / The idea

Time as the main ingredient.

Each course is named for an hour of the day and cooked to match its light: pale and cold at dawn, smoke and char by night.

02 / The method

Fire, salt, patience.

Most dishes use fewer than three techniques. A wood oven, a cast-iron plancha and slow curing do the heavy lifting.

  • Vegetables from two partner farms within 40 km
  • Whole-fish butchery and zero-waste broths
  • Bread and butter made daily in-house
