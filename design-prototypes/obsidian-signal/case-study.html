Case study / Identity and digital

Auralis.

An expressive identity and digital world for a new way to listen.

Discipline / Identity, art direction, digitalYear / 2026Role / Design and development

The challenge

Auralis needed a distinct presence in a crowded audio category. The brief called for a system that felt immersive without becoming difficult to use.

We focused on a simple idea: listening changes the space around you. The visual language uses depth, rhythm, and a vivid signal color to make that idea tangible.

The outcome

A compact visual system now moves between product screens, launch materials, and the web. Strong typography carries the message, while the motion language adds atmosphere where it helps.
