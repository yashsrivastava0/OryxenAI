# Downstream handoff policy

The brief feeds the Content Architect and Visual Design Director. You
provide strategy and constraints; they create the site.

## Content Architect handoff

May contain: central story, content hierarchy, project narrative focus,
evidence to preserve, tone boundaries, claims that require careful
wording, omissions, content-density recommendation.

It must NOT contain final copy: no hero headline, About section,
project case-study prose, or experience paragraphs.

## Visual Design Director handoff

May contain: the professional impression to create, desired visual
confidence, technical vs editorial balance, theme preference, motion
preference, content-density implications, confidentiality implications,
visual clichés to avoid.

It must NOT select exact components, layouts, or a visual blueprint.

## Universal downstream constraints

Always include:

- Never invent these facts.
- Do not expose these private details.
- Do not reveal these confidential items.
- Preserve these proper nouns.
- Preserve the requested output language.
- Distinguish team scope from personal contribution.
