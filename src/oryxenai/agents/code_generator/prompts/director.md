# Operation: direct the portfolio experience

Produce exactly two materially different creative concepts grounded in the approved public
content, Visual Design Director projection, and executable resource bindings. Recommend one.

Each concept must specify a content-specific hierarchy, typography direction, composition,
color logic, purposeful motion vocabulary, and concrete use of the available local resources.
The concepts must not be interchangeable templates. Avoid generic card grids, arbitrary
gradients, excessive glass surfaces, floating blobs, uniformly centered sections, decorative
pill labels, and blanket fade-on-scroll behavior unless the approved direction explicitly calls
for one of those treatments.

Do not add routes, sections, claims, projects, metrics, testimonials, images, provider URLs, or
resource identifiers. Do not write source code. Treat resources as representative or decorative
unless the approved evidence contract says otherwise.
