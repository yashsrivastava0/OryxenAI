How I work

Clarity first, then craft — in that order.

A short loop of listening, testing and simplifying, repeated until the work is obvious.

  1. Find the real problem

    Interviews, shadowing and data before any pixels, so the team agrees what is broken.

  2. Design the smallest thing

    Prototype early, test with real users and ship the simplest version that works.

  3. Make it a system

    Reusable patterns and clear documentation so the next ten screens take a day.

  4. Stay for the outcome

    Work with engineers through launch and measure whether the change helped people.

Case study

Clinician dashboard

A calmer home screen for nurses working across dozens of patients.

Role
Lead designer
Period
2023

Conceptual artwork — illustrative, not a product screenshot.

Problem

Nurses lost minutes per patient switching between four screens to find the next task, and critical alerts were buried among routine notices.

Approach

We shadowed eighteen nurses across three hospitals, mapped the real handoff moments and rebuilt the dashboard around one prioritised queue with clear alert tiers, prototyping and testing every change on the ward.

Outcome

Charting time fell by a third and alert response improved in all three pilot hospitals.

Case study

Merchant onboarding

A guided setup that took small merchants from signup to first payment.

Role
Product designer
Period
2021

Conceptual artwork — illustrative, not a product screenshot.

Problem

Small merchants abandoned onboarding when asked for documents they did not have to hand.

Approach

We split the flow into saveable steps, explained why each document mattered and let merchants take payments before verification finished.

Outcome

Completed signups rose sharply within the first quarter.
