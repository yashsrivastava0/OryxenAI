Privacy

{{ app_name }} is a resume and portfolio demonstration project. This page describes how the workspace uses your information.

Google sign-in

Google and Supabase authenticate your account. We use your Google account identifier and email address to create and recognize your workspace account. Google may also provide basic profile information, such as your name. We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Google Drive, or your contacts.

Your portfolio materials

We store the account information, materials, answers, content plans, chat edits, and generated pages needed to run your workspace. Materials you submit for generation are processed by the model providers configured for this project. Google sign-in information is used for authentication rather than as portfolio source material.

Access and storage

Your workspace and portfolio preview are private to your account. Project administrators can manage accounts and service records. Supabase provides authentication and database storage, Render hosts the application, and configured model providers process generation requests. Browser session storage keeps you signed in.

Removal and questions

Contact the project operator through the support email shown on the Google consent screen to ask about your account or request removal. Account and workspace records remain until they are removed through the project's cleanup and account lifecycle processes; historical records may follow the configured retention policy.
